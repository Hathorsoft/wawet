# Code of conduct

We welcome contributors of every background and skill level. Be respectful,
constructive and precise; challenge ideas without attacking people. Explain
technical tradeoffs and recognise corrections. No harassment, discrimination,
threats, doxxing, sexualised conduct or deliberate disruption.

This policy applies to project repositories, issue discussions, reviews and
project-organised activities. Maintainers may remove content, pause discussions,
warn participants or restrict participation according to severity and repeated
behaviour. Apply rules fairly, including to maintainers; respect confidentiality.

Report concerns privately to a project maintainer through their published
contact channel. If a complaint concerns a maintainer, contact another
maintainer where available. The project currently has no dedicated confidential
reporting mailbox or independent appeals panel; establish these before growing
community operations. Do not publish another person's personal information.

This is an original concise project policy, not a claim of membership in an
external enforcement programme. A reporter should be told what next steps and
limits to confidentiality apply before further details are requested.
