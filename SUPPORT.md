# Support

This is an early research project, with no commercial appliance or support SLA.
Use repository issues for reproducible software problems, documented experiment
results and focused questions. Search existing issues and include Python/OS,
revision, command and redacted output. Do not post vehicle tracks or identities.

Start with [the README](README.md) and [contribution guide](CONTRIBUTING.md).
Hardware and regulatory research is not product support or permission to use
a transmitter. Safety-critical or sensitive issues follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
Official commercial support/warranty terms will be defined if hardware launches;
third-party builds must identify their own supplier and support arrangements.
