# Wawet Pocket — future device class

Portable node for hiking, personal communication and experimentation with a
phone/laptop. Share application protocols and compatible bearer interfaces;
do not assume a Drive enclosure or power source. Battery runtime, GNSS, antenna,
weather resistance and portable identity privacy need independent requirements.
Messaging may use LXMF rather than inventing a second message service. No
Pocket hardware, firmware or battery design exists in this iteration.
