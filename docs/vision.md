# Vision

Wawet aims to make resilient civilian communications inexpensive enough for
ordinary people to deploy. Road reporting is the first application, not the
boundary of the ecosystem. Wawet is the project and technology brand;
Hathorsoft Ltd is its principal maintainer and intended official manufacturer.

**Design principle:** sophisticated network, simple edge device. Phones and
gateways handle rich interfaces, language processing, maps and observability.
An edge device carries only what useful basic offline operation requires.
No mandatory account, cloud authentication, central routing or subscription.

A consumer should plug in a complete appliance with its antenna supplied,
mount it safely, optionally pair a phone, and report a common hazard by touch.
They should not need to know radio frequencies, RNode or Reticulum.

Longer-term applications include rural/community communication, temporary
networks, hiking, sensors, home integration and long-distance infrastructure.
Neither LoRa nor cars belong in the core application's transport assumptions.

**Assumptions to test:** enough nearby users or infrastructure exist to make
reports valuable; brief encounters permit affordable radio delivery; a small
standalone device can process events; and trustworthy enough reports can be
obtained without tracking drivers or imposing central accounts. Failure of
these assumptions can change the product or underlying architecture.

“Wawet — Open the way.” is a provisional tagline. No finished commercial
product or availability date is promised.
