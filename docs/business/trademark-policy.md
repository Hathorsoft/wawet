# Provisional brand and trademark policy

Wawet is a modern working name inspired by Wepwawet. It is not itself a literal
translation of “Opener of the Ways”. Hathorsoft Ltd maintains the project and
intends to manufacture official hardware. “Wawet — Open the way.” is provisional.
Formal name/trademark clearance and registration have not been completed; no
registered trademark status is claimed.

Copyright licences permit use, modification and commercial distribution within
their scope; they do not grant an endorsement or official product identity.
Third parties may truthfully describe products as “compatible with Wawet” when
supported by interoperability evidence and without suggesting Hathorsoft approval.
Use your own product/company branding. Do not label independent products
“official Wawet hardware” or use project logos as endorsement without permission.

Do not make technical interoperability conditional on trademark permission.
During experimental protocol development, state the tested version and avoid
unqualified production compatibility claims. No compatibility certification or
logo programme exists yet. Terms for any future programme require review.

Wire magic, version and field semantics should survive a brand rename. Do not
embed marketing/tagline strings in packet formats. Review this policy before
commercial launch with the jurisdictions and marks actually cleared.
