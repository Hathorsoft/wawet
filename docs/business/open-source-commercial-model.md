# Open-source commercial model

Hathorsoft intends to sell the most convenient official appliance: assembled
and tested electronics, reliable RF/antenna, enclosure, mounts, trusted update
packages, regulatory documentation, support and warranty. Community members
can inspect, contribute, fork, build compatible devices and sell compliant
products under the applicable licences. Do not sabotage self-builders.

**DESIGN DECISION:** no compulsory consumer subscription or cloud service.
If Hathorsoft disappears, compatible devices must still communicate. Optional
future fleet/support services need separate evidence of customer value, not
assumed recurring revenue. No such service exists now.

Reciprocal hardware licensing supports shared improvements; trademarks distinguish
official products but do not prohibit truthful compatibility descriptions. Neither
mechanism guarantees commercial success or prevents cheaper lawful competitors.
[Arduino's policy](https://www.arduino.cc/en/trademark/) is an example of separating
open design from official branding, not a prediction of Wawet profitability.

Test consumer willingness to pay and participation density before custom tooling.
Track total delivered costs, support load, channel margin and warranty returns.
Supplier quotations, conformity programme and realistic volume must replace
[paper cost assumptions](../../devices/drive/docs/hardware-concepts.md).
Protect reliability and user experience rather than adding proprietary routing,
locked firmware or required accounts. OEM/enterprise arrangements must not
contradict contributor rights or upstream licensing obligations.
